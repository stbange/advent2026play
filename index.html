24 DOORS · ONE WINTER

ADVENT 2026

A winter hall of original retro worlds.

Move with WASD. Look with the mouse. Click to fire. Press E to use a portal or valve.

On touch screens, drag the left side to move and the right side to look. Use the Fire and Use buttons.

All 24 worlds have playable versions for up to four friends. Local difficulty applies to new expeditions; a saved expedition keeps its mode. Online difficulty is set by the server host. Enable preview before December. Online players must select the world configured on their friend’s server.

Ready when you are.

Day One: 1–8 select weapons; C kicks or detonates planted charges; Q toggles the jetpack after finding it. Touch: GUNS opens the arsenal. Keyboard: WASD move; E use; Space jump; Q alternate; C command; Tab next; Backspace return. R/F ascend/descend. Controller: left stick move, right stick look, trigger fire, west use, south jump, north command, east next, back return; D-pad up/down vertical. Start pauses that player's input.
