All 24 doors now have a concept-art pass. Look for the quarry derrick in Crystal Front, the glass roof and clock in Last Train, the hanging bell in Black Bell Keep and the Ferris wheel in Carnival of Ashes. Each world uses its own materials, equipment and encounter silhouettes. [Compare the paintings with the player views](https://github.com/stbange/advent2026/tree/main/verification/anthology-concept-art).

**Day Two — Emberwake: Glasswind Oasis.** Fly a carpet over a dusk desert, through an aqueduct and into roofed caverns. Hunt twelve mana monsters, claim their drops and escort your balloon against one rival wizard. Bank 180 of the world's conserved 240 mana to win. Summon an enterable castle, upgrade it through three tiers, defend its covered heart and break the rival's gate to raid its bank. Five spells support solo and 1–4-player co-op; two local views share the world. Enable **Preview all available doors**, select **02 — Emberwake** and use Door 2. **1** Firebolt, **2** Castle (hold Fire for one second; **C** rotates), **3** Shield, **4** Earth Rise; **Q/CLAIM** always claims mana. Touch **SPELLS** selects the spell. [Rules and controls](https://github.com/stbange/advent2026/blob/867e8896b6322d8e04736a2370a3e41890918be3/docs/emberwake.md) · [Implementation and tests](https://github.com/stbange/advent2026/tree/867e8896b6322d8e04736a2370a3e41890918be3/verification/emberwake-mana-war). Existing incompatible Day Two expeditions restart; earned calendar ornaments are preserved.

**Replay Day One.** Midnight Matinee generates 36 combinations of open passages through the street, alley, auditorium and backstage. Weapons and alien encounter positions vary. The cinema landmarks, rooftop objectives and Producer finale return in every run. Finish, return to the hall and enter the first portal again for a different layout. The party shares its generated route across saves and reconnects. Older active Day One checkpoints may restart through save recovery. Earned ornaments stay on the profile. [Route comparison and verification](https://github.com/stbange/advent2026/tree/867e8896b6322d8e04736a2370a3e41890918be3/verification/matinee-procedural-routes).

**Day One is Midnight Matinee — No Refunds:** a connected cinema district with eight guns, four variants per alien type, moving scenery, four environmental problems, seven secrets, forty subtitle jokes and the three-phase Producer finale. It retains approved ElevenLabs music/effects and adds five documented weapon-sound derivatives. The other 23 early episodes remain available.

Read the [Day One design](https://github.com/stbange/advent2026/blob/867e8896b6322d8e04736a2370a3e41890918be3/docs/midnight-matinee.md) and [verification](https://github.com/stbange/advent2026/tree/867e8896b6322d8e04736a2370a3e41890918be3/verification/midnight-matinee). Its **20-minute first-play duration is a design target awaiting human playtests**. Fresh solo Classic and four-player Veteran automated completion and checkpoint/reward checks pass.

Enable **Preview all available doors** before December. Otherwise doors open daily at 00:00 UTC from 1–24 December 2026. Choose a world and difficulty, then enter the hall. Click or tap the game view to enable sound; volume controls are in Settings. No ElevenLabs account or runtime service is required.

Day One: 1–8 select weapons; Tab cycles; C kicks or detonates owned charges; Q toggles the acquired jetpack; R/F fly vertically. Touch players can use the GUNS selector.

Use WASD/mouse, click to fire, E to use, Space to jump, Q alternate, C command, Tab next and R/F vertical. Near Crystal Front's tank, C boards and Q dismounts. Phones show touch controls. The world renders within 384 × 240 pixels while the interface stays readable at display resolution.

Offline play and two local views need no server. Online rooms require a separately hosted authoritative server and matching content; enter its WSS address in the lobby. GitHub Pages hosts this static client.
