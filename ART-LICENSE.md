# Original art license policy

Original artwork, models, textures, sprites, music, sound effects and visual level content are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

Generated ElevenLabs audio is an exception: it uses [LicenseRef-ElevenLabsMedia](licenses/ElevenLabsMedia.txt) for use within the game, with the credit **Created in collaboration with ElevenLabs**. Audio ranges in mixed scene buffers are identified by `audio.json` and `audio-source.json`. Those audio portions are excluded from CC BY 4.0 and MIT; original geometry in the same buffer retains CC BY 4.0. The original synthesized fallback WAVs retain CC BY 4.0.

- Legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.txt

The slice contains original map layouts, palette/font data, textured hall/foundry meshes, prop and weapon models, directional sprite animations and synthesized audio. See [asset attribution](assets/ATTRIBUTION.md) and [editable source notes](art/source/README.md). The [51 original hall and episode concepts](art/concepts/README.md) also use CC BY 4.0; their manifest and adjacent JSON files contain creator, generation and source-design records. Production art for the remaining anthology remains separate work. Do not apply this license to third-party or classic-game assets. Preserve creator attribution, source, license link and modification notices for every accepted asset. Lua, shaders, the Python art exporter and other code remain MIT even when stored beside art.
